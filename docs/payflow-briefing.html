AP Decision Engine · Case-Study Briefing

PayFlow

From a vendor invoice to a decision you can defend.

A vendor emails a messy PDF invoice. PayFlow reads it, checks it against the purchase order, the goods receipt, the vendor master, and the company's spend history — then returns one of three answers, APPROVE / HOLD / REJECT, with every number it compared shown in plain English. It auto-clears the safe majority and hands a human a pre-investigated exception, not a black box.

The job isn't “extract the fields.” It's making a defensible decision under messy inputs — and being able to explain it to the person who has to act on it.

How it decides

Four principles run underneath every verdict. They're the whole point — the judgment, not the plumbing.

Principle 01

Bias to HOLD when unsure

A wrong auto-approve loses real money that's hard to claw back; an unnecessary hold costs a clerk ~2 minutes. So when uncertain: hold. REJECT is reserved for the unambiguously wrong.

Principle 02

Confidence × Materiality

Scrutiny scales on two axes at once — how sure the extraction is, and how much money is on the line. A $40 invoice and a $400K invoice are not checked the same way.

Principle 03

Most-severe gate wins

Gates run in order and accumulate. One red flag beats ten green checks — a clean invoice from an unapproved vendor still holds.

Principle 04

Always cite the numbers

Never “held for review.” Instead: “subtotal $10,300 vs PO $10,000 = +3%, over the ±1% tolerance.” Auditable by design.

The demo — the invoices we'll show, live

Each is dragged into the app and read by Gemini on the spot (never seen before). Together they span a clean approval, real 3-way depth, a financial control, a fraud instinct, a policy call, and a timing judgment — six different kinds of decision. Each is read by Gemini live, on the spot.

Approve
Acme Corporation Inc. · PO-1001
$12,000
Clean 3-way match — invoice = PO = goods receipt, within tolerance. Straight-through, no human needed.
Hold
Stark Logistics LLC · PO-1004
$10,300 + $800 tax
The goods are 3% over the PO — beyond the ±1% / $25 tolerance. Tax & freight are excluded from the comparison; only the goods variance trips it.
Hold
Cyberdyne Systems Corp · PO-1006
$40,000
Matches the PO and the receipt exactly — but it's ~20× this vendor's normal spend. Flagged as an anomaly before anyone pays it.
Approve + notify
Umbrella Facilities Corp · no PO
$385
No PO needed — under the $500 bypass limit for a trusted, bypass-approved vendor. Auto-approved, and a note fires to Finance so nothing is silent.
Approve
Hooli Cloud Services · PO-1013
$10,000
Received in two shipments ($4k + $6k). PayFlow sums the goods receipts and matches — a naive matcher, seeing only the first receipt, would wrongly reject it. Real 3-way depth.
Hold
Pied Piper Data Corp · PO-1014
$8,000
The invoice arrived before the goods receipt. Held awaiting receipt — not rejected; the goods may be in transit. A timing/lifecycle call, not a mismatch.

What to say in the room

  • The real AP bottleneck isn't fraud — it's GL coding (which budget each invoice hits). I built that in and gate it by confidence. Most people miss it.
  • I optimised for the cost of being wrong, not accuracy in a vacuum — that's why the system leans to HOLD.
  • I scoped out OFAC screening, 1099 tracking, and month-end accruals on purpose — and I can point to exactly where each would plug in.
  • The goal metric is straight-through-processing rate — auto-clear the safe ~80%, catch 100% of the risky ones, and make every hold resolvable in seconds.
